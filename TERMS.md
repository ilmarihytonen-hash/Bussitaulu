## Data and privacy

The app has no user account or app-operated server. Favorites, language choice, interface scale, nearby-area radius, location preference, and up to 30 days of observed arrival history are stored in this browser on this device. Arrival history is grouped by Europe/Helsinki dates and starts a new daily record at Helsinki midnight. Current location coordinates are held only in memory while the page is open and are not saved by Bussitaulu.

If you grant browser location permission, the app uses your coordinates to find nearby stops and request local weather. Weather requests send the coordinates to Open-Meteo. The app requests transit data from Föli and map tiles and approximate traffic-signal data from OpenStreetMap-related services. These requests go directly from your browser to those providers, which may process them under their own privacy policies and terms.
